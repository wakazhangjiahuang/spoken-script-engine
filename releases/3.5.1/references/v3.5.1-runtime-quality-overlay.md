## Remaining v3.5.0 execution gap
The v3.5.0 entrypoint runs the content-payoff gate but its final validate/export step delegates to the v3.4.2 base entrypoint. That base path calls the lightweight validate_ai_video_review(), not the formal validate_v41.py validator. Consequently V4.1 review routing, generator/reviewer identity comparison, weighted total, full-validation state and --require-v41-full-validation are not fully enforced by the sole v3.5.0 entrypoint.

## v3.5.1 fix
1. Add formal_v41_gate_v351.py: bind the AI-video review to exact batch-file SHA or normalized payload SHA, then call validate_v41.validate(batch, review).
2. Require at least one genuinely independent reviewer; incomplete fast_screen, deep_review or critic routing blocks editorial release.
3. Make --require-v41-full-validation effective: V4.1 final status must be PASS. If real voice or 3–5 person blind-read evidence is absent, remain VALIDATION_PENDING.
4. Keep all v3.5.0 content gates: source need, three distinct entries, worked-example before/after, observable delta, transfer rule, first-eight progression, Calling value exchange, critical score floors and negative regressions.
5. Illustration remains on V2.0 independent review, per-dimension scoring and six-sheet XLSX validation.
