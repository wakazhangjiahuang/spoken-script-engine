# spoken-script-engine v3.6.0｜Source-first marketing quality repair

## Release scope
A targeted repair on top of v3.5.1. Preserve AI Video V4.1, Illustration V2.0, existing audiences, source priorities, title guide, six-sheet illustration workbook, v3.5.0 content-payoff checks and v3.5.1 formal V4.1 wiring. Do not expand into new business lines or replace the current knowledge base.

## 1. Two separate benchmark classes
- Illustration commercial-case benchmark: a user-provided commercial illustration project. Its source status remains USER_PROVIDED; external proof is not implied. Extract buyer/product specificity, brief translation, professional design decisions, tool-as-support, original artwork-to-product translation, commercial outcome and portfolio-level next value.
- AI Video A01: a calibrated livestream-replay marketing-method reference, not performance-verified. Extract audience identity, cognitive contrast, information gap, concrete value preview, workflow payoff, capability amplification and next-level value.

Both are mechanism references only. Never score them as derived scripts, use them as automatic few-shot copy, mix domains or copy their exact wording. Each plan must explicitly record benchmark use/non-use, selected mechanisms, transfer rationale, new application and non-transferable elements.

## 2. Source-first intake and case provenance
brief.source_inventory is mandatory before planning. It records checked assets with exact asset ID, locator, source quote and read=true, relevant real project case IDs, selected real case IDs, excluded relevant cases with reasons, and the source-selection rationale. If no materials exist, record status=no_source_materials and a concrete reason.

Default case policy is prefer_real. Relevant user-project facts with status provided or verified must be selected or explicitly rejected with a reason. A real commercial case cannot be relabeled simulated. Hypothetical scenarios require explicit user authorization or an allowed no-fit decision after all relevant real cases have been excluded with reasons; disclosure must appear in spoken script.

## 3. Content-mode routing
Each plan selects one mode and supplies its matching story plan:
- real_commercial_case: buyer need, professional judgment, tool/method, product translation, result, transferable value.
- ai_workflow_tutorial: original input, AI process, human judgment, actual output, application value.
- design_decision_analysis: product brief, design constraints, tradeoff, product fit, decision rule.
- business_opportunity_analysis: user opportunity, market evidence, business logic, risk boundary, action recommendation.
- hypothetical_scenario: simulation disclosure, assumptions, decision path, illustrative output and boundary.

The worked-example contract remains, but no universal A/B-before/after story is forced onto every topic.

## 4. Marketing-quality review bound to actual speech
The independent reviewer must evaluate and quote-bind: user relevance; click chain and new information in 3–8 seconds; promise/payoff; content-mode fit; capability amplification and human/AI boundary where applicable; next-level value; source fidelity. Quotes must occur in the actual script. Reviewer identity must differ from generator identity and must not be a same-model role switch.

Capability claims such as one person/small team can do it, reduce labor or replace a team require an available demonstration/proof resource. Otherwise the claim is blocked or must be removed.

The deterministic gate validates provenance, mode, evidence references and review bindings. It does not certify actual persuasion, click-through, conversion or platform performance.

## 5. Runtime wiring
The sole entrypoint is scripts/run_pipeline_v3_6.py. It runs the v3.5.0 content gate, v3.5.1 formal V4.1 gate and v3.6.0 source/marketing gate at intake, plan, draft and validate/export boundaries. Regression suite: test_content_quality_v350.py, test_formal_v41_gate_v351.py, test_marketing_quality_gate_v360.py, test_release_alignment_v360.py.
