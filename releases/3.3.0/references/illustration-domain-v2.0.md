# Illustration Domain Contract — V2.0

## Sample classes
- `BENCHMARK`: immutable positive reference. Extract mechanisms only; never score, diagnose, rewrite or include in the derived-script batch.
- `DERIVED_SCRIPT`: independently planned and written content; eligible for per-script scoring and review.
- `STRUCTURAL_REFERENCE_ONLY`: workbook/layout fixture; may validate fields and mappings but is not a positive copywriting example.
- `CANDIDATE` and `HISTORICAL`: not default few-shot material.

## Fixed production contract
- 18 derived scripts: 6 Douyin, 6 WeChat Channels, 6 Xiaohongshu.
- Six workbook sheets: `00_使用总览`, `01_抖音`, `02_视频号`, `03_小红书`, `04_拍摄执行卡`, `05_AI数字人口播执行卡`.
- Four user-locked fields are read-only and compared exactly against the source workbook by Script_ID and platform. Any mismatch blocks export.
- 04 contains six segments per script: 108 rows total.
- 05 has a dynamic number of shots; each generated shot is 4–15 seconds and maps to a Script_ID and 04 segment ID.
- Real evidence assets remain real assets and are inserted in post; generated presenters must not fabricate artwork, product proof, customer messages, receipts or official documents.

## Expression contract
Every script plan records: audience voice, shared viewer task, current belief, desired outcome, core tension, at least three materially different entry candidates, selected angle, click reason, promise/payoff, proof plan, domain-specific professional judgement, transferable value, next value and forbidden claims.

## Content logic
Build from the evidence available:
1. Material: original brief, product, trend source, survey scope, artwork, test, official source or user-provided project.
2. Professional judgement: line, palette, character recognition, composition, series rhythm, repeat density, substrate/process fit and rights boundaries.
3. Transfer: a usable next action such as portfolio improvement, product adaptation, proposal structure, series development or research workflow.

## Title governance
The cover visual, main title and subtitle form one marketing unit. Their roles are flexible. Do not force the main title to be a benefit statement or the subtitle to be proof only. Select the entry from the material and shared viewer task before platform presentation.

## Evidence
Separate VERIFIED, USER_PROVIDED, PUBLIC_SOURCE, INFERENCE and UNKNOWN/NOT_RUN. A locked text field is never silently rewritten to hide an evidence gap; route the issue to a publish gate.
