# spoken-script-engine v3.6.1

Patch release on v3.6.0. It closes one quality-gate loophole: 3–8 second bridge/suspense text could previously be quoted as if it were the body payoff. The v3.6.1 gate restricts promise/payoff and content-mode evidence anchors to body roles only, and adds a regression test proving the bridge cannot substitute for body delivery. All v3.6.0 source-first, benchmark governance, content-mode, independent-review, AI Video V4.1 and Illustration V2.0 rules remain unchanged.
