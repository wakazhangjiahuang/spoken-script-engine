# spoken-script-engine v3.6.1

Targeted repair on v3.5.1. Preserve AI Video V4.1, Illustration V2.0, current audiences, source assets, platform-specific production rules and the existing workbook contracts.

## Source-first intake
Before planning, inventory the current user materials, original project sources, source facts, production resources and current domain guidance. Record source_inventory. Default to prefer_real: use relevant real user projects before hypothetical examples. Every relevant real case must be selected or explicitly rejected with a reason. Simulation requires authorization or an allowed no-fit decision and spoken disclosure.

## Separate benchmark classes
- illustration_commercial_case: commercial illustration case mechanisms—buyer context, brief translation, design judgment, tool as support, original artwork-to-product translation, source-bounded result and portfolio-level next value.
- ai_video_marketing_method: AI-video A01 mechanisms—audience identity, cognitive contrast, information gap, concrete value preview, workflow payoff, capability amplification and next-level value.

Both are mechanism references only, not scored scripts or automatic few-shot examples. Do not copy wording or mix domains.

## Content-mode routing
Each plan selects one mode: real_commercial_case, ai_workflow_tutorial, design_decision_analysis, business_opportunity_analysis or authorized hypothetical_scenario. Use the matching story structure; do not force every topic into an A/B-before/after narrative.

- **正文兑现锚点：** Promise/payoff and content-mode evidence must be quoted from actual body roles only; the 3–8 second bridge cannot substitute for body payoff.

## Independent marketing review
Every script is reviewed against actual spoken quotes for user relevance, click chain and 3–8 second new information, promise/payoff, content-mode fit, capability amplification and human/AI boundary, next-level value, and source fidelity. Reviewer identity must be independent of the generator. Field presence alone is not a pass.

## Existing domain gates remain
AI Video uses the V4.1 formal validator, review routing, exact batch SHA and original scorecard. Illustration uses V2.0 independent review, locked fields, rights checks and six-sheet workbook validation. Never merge their facts, benchmarks, templates or score weights.

External human voice test, target-user blind read, rights/permissions and platform outcome remain VALIDATION_PENDING until evidenced. CI success does not certify market performance.
